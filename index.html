How to play

⚽ In a matchTap a team-mate to pass. Swipe towards goal to shoot. Hold your finger on the pitch to run there. Without the ball, swipe to slide tackle.
👆 Swipe to shootIn the shooting games, put your finger on the ball and flick towards where you want it to go. A short swipe keeps it low, a long one goes high.
💨 Speed = powerFlick fast to blast it past the keeper — but too hard and it might fly over.
🌀 Curve itBend your swipe like a banana to curl the ball round the wall.
🧤 Be the keeperIn the Penalty Cup you also save penalties: tap the spot in the goal where you want to dive. Tap early to guess, or late to react!
